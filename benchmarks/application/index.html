Method

All frameworks render the same semantic landmarks and 120 product records through their public client APIs. Warm-ups precede interleaved samples. Each update action is measured after its framework-specific scheduler settles; correctness checks validate product count, keyed boundaries, selected detail, configuration, bag state, and teardown.
